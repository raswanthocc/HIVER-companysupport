Human Handoff Policy & Safety Rules

Account Security & Fraud

Inquiries involving unauthorized logins, hacked accounts, fraud, or identity compromise are immediately transferred to human security agents.

Triggers: hacked, stolen, unauthorized, compromised, fraud
Lost & Stolen Cards

Card cancellations and stolen card blocks require multi-factor human authorization for customer financial protection.

Triggers: lost card, stolen card, block card, freeze card
Hardware Safety & Fire Hazards

Physical hazards such as smoking batteries, sparking devices, or electrical shocks bypass AI to reach emergency repair teams.

Triggers: smoke, spark, catch fire, explode, swollen battery
PII & Credential Protection

Sharing sensitive credentials (SSN, credit card numbers, passwords) automatically routes to a secure private human DM.

Triggers: SSN, credit card number, password, CVV
Legal Disputes

Legal actions or attorney communications are handled directly by specialized legal support representatives.

Triggers: lawyer, lawsuit, attorney, court action
